TimeTracker — Redesign Concept

A ground-up rethink of the mobile app. One job done well: start, stop, and account for your time in seconds. Five tabs, large targets, zero clutter — every screen below. The bloated CRM / kanban / deals surface moves out of the primary app; core tracking, timesheets and reports are front and center.
